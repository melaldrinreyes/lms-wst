# How Students Can View Course Content

## ✅ Everything is Set Up!

Your LMS is fully configured for students to view course content. Here's how it works:

## 📍 Steps for Students to View Content

### 1. **Go to Your Course**
   - Log in to your student account
   - Navigate to "My Courses"
   - Click on any course

### 2. **Find the Content Tab**
   - Look at the tabs at the top of the course page
   - You should see: Overview | **Content** | Modules | Assignments | Announcements
   - If content exists, the "Content" tab will show a blue **✓** checkmark

### 3. **Click Content Tab**
   - Click the "Content" tab
   - If the instructor has posted content, you'll see it displayed beautifully formatted

### 4. **View Content**
   - Read formatted text
   - Watch embedded YouTube videos
   - View images
   - See tables with data
   - Click links to resources

## 📊 Content Display

When viewing course content, you'll see:

✓ **Rich Text Formatting**
- Headings, bold, italic text
- Bullet points and numbered lists
- Blockquotes and colored text

✓ **Media**
- 🎥 YouTube videos (plays directly in the course)
- 🖼️ Images (displays in full quality)
- 📊 Tables with proper formatting
- 🔗 Clickable links to resources

✓ **Professional Layout**
- Clean, dark theme
- Easy to read
- Mobile-friendly (works on phone, tablet, desktop)
- Fast loading

## 🎯 Quick Access

### From Overview Tab
- A special "Course Content" card appears in the Overview tab
- Click it to jump directly to course materials

### From Course List
- Look for the blue **✓** indicator on courses that have content
- This shows content is available

## 🔄 Updates

- When your instructor posts content, it appears instantly
- No page refresh needed (though you may need to click back and re-open)
- All updates are live in real-time

## ❓ What If There's No Content?

If the instructor hasn't posted content yet:
- You'll see a message: "No Course Content Yet"
- Check back later - content will appear as soon as the instructor posts it

## 🎓 What Can Instructors Share?

Your instructors can create:
- Course syllabus and guidelines
- Lecture notes with formatting
- Video tutorials embedded directly
- Data tables and charts
- Links to external resources
- Detailed course schedules

## 📱 Mobile Access

You can view course content on:
- ✓ Desktop computers
- ✓ Tablets
- ✓ Mobile phones (iOS and Android)
- ✓ Any modern web browser

Content automatically adjusts to your screen size!

## ⚡ Performance

- Fast loading times
- Smooth scrolling
- Professional appearance
- Responsive design

## 🔐 Access

- Only students enrolled in the course can view content
- Each course has its own separate content
- Your access is controlled by course enrollment
